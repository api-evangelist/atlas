Few professions are as synonymous with the word “accountability” as accounting. The profession takes its responsibilities seriously, and mistakes carry real liability: every return, every opinion is signed by an accountant. The final output that clients pay for is the assurance that someone qualified has reviewed the work and stands behind it.
