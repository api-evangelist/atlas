If you’re a firm owner and you’ve looked into AI seriously, you’ve probably run into four potential deployment paths: Point solutions that bloat your tech stack; more than a dozen tools, none sharing context, and your team stitching them together. Custom software built by agencies who stick to scope, focus on looking good, and leave right after delivery. In-house AI teams that only make financial sense at the scale of Big 4.
